We never split gross sales.

Every payment follows the same order, and every party sees the same statement. Nobody's share is worked out from a number that still has tax, refunds or costs inside it.

  1. Taxes come off first.
  2. Then refunds and cancellations.
  3. Then payment and booking charges.
  4. Then the direct costs agreed in advance, such as product cost, delivery, maintenance or insurance.
  5. What remains is shared between host, partner and platform at the agreed percentages.

Questions owners ask first

Do I have to invest anything?

Most opportunities are partner-funded or low-capital: the partner supplies the stock, equipment or expertise. Some need small costs on your side, such as electricity, a permit or minor fit-out. Your report lists these before you decide.

How does Partnermybusiness earn?

We take an agreed share of the new revenue, so we earn when you earn. Any set-up cost for a specific opportunity is shown in your report before you commit.

Is the income guaranteed?

No. The dashboard shows projections from example figures that you can change. You earn a share of actual sales, and each stream has a target and a review date.

Will it disturb my regular customers?

You approve every opportunity, its design, its schedule and its brand. Activity is planned for slow days and quiet hours, and your peak times stay protected.

Why do doctors, lawyers and accountants work differently?

Their professional rules bar referral commissions and fee sharing. Hospitals, clinics and pharmacies may not pay or take commission per patient or prescription, and lawyers and accountants may not pay for referrals. Those collaborations use room hire, facility fees, workshops and flat listing fees instead. We confirm the rules for your city before anything launches.

Who handles the money?

Customers pay through platform-approved digital checkout. Taxes, refunds, fees and agreed costs come off first, and each party's share is calculated from the same statement. Payment set-up is approved separately for each country.

What if a stream does not work?

We change the offer, pricing or display, replace the partner, or remove the stream. You can end a single stream with notice without ending your whole agreement.

Who is responsible if a product or machine causes a problem?

The partner is responsible for its products, equipment and staff, and carries the insurance for them. You remain responsible for your premises and your core business. This is written into every opportunity sheet.

I am a professional or a brand, not a venue. How do I join?

Choose your profession at the top of the page to see where you could work and with whom, then send your details. After verification you sign one agreement and become available to every suitable business in the network.
